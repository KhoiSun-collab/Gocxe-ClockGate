Gate tablet

Every scan is saved on the tablet first, with the time of the scan. When the tablet gets a connection, scans are sent on. A hardware QR scanner that types into the page (USB or Bluetooth) also works at the gate.

Card security and pairing

Every card QR is signed with a private company key. QR codes made anywhere else, or for another business, are rejected at the gate. Pair each gate tablet once with the pairing QR and keep it private. Cards you print later work on every paired tablet, however many workers you add.
